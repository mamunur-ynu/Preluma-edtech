# Product vision

Preluma helps students prepare before class and helps teachers see where support is needed. The core experience is a five-stage mission: Brain Brief, example, practice, mock test and overview. The platform supports curated topic packs, courses, homework, projects, an AI tutor and role-specific dashboards.

Primary users are university students, teachers and a small set of administrators. A successful supervised pilot would show that learners can complete preparation, understand why a lesson is recommended, and receive useful feedback, while teachers can verify the underlying evidence and protect student data.

Current non-goals: an unapproved public deployment, claims of measured learning efficacy, automatic Next.js migration, unsupported learning-style diagnosis, and costly speculative features. Maintain the modular Streamlit product until a measured need justifies another frontend.

Success signals to measure in a future authorized pilot: pre/post readiness, mission completion, return use, helpfulness of explanations, teacher trust, privacy incidents, p95 latency and cost per AI session. No real-pilot measurements exist yet.
